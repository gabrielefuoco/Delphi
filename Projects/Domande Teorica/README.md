# Domande Teorica

## Overview
This project, "Domande Teorica," is designed to collate, structure, and analyze theoretical questions for an academic syllabus. The goal is to provide a comprehensive, formally sound repository of concepts, logically categorized for optimal cognitive retention and academic review.

## Planned Structure
Currently awaiting further instructions to construct the detailed taxonomy. The expected structure will involve:
1. **Core Fundamentals**: Definition of the foundational entities and concepts.
2. **Advanced Principles**: Deep dives into complex interactions, mechanisms, and models.
3. **Formal Analysis**: Mathematical or formal proofs as required by the specific theoretical framework.

The `chunks.json` will be generated dynamically based on the upcoming topic specifications and available literature.
