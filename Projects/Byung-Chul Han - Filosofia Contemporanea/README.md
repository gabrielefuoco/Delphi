# Byung-Chul Han - Contemporary Philosophy Handout

This project explores the thought of the philosopher Byung-Chul Han, analyzing his diagnosis of contemporary society.
Through a review of his main works (The Burnout Society, The Transparency Society, Psychopolitics, The Expulsion of the Other, etc.), the project aims to outline the transition from the disciplinary society to the achievement society and digital control.

## Project Structure

The project is divided into the following chapters:

1. Introduction: Byung-Chul Han and the Diagnosis of the Present
2. From the Disciplinary Society to the Achievement Society
3. The Burnout Society: Self-Exploitation and Exhaustion
4. The Expulsion of the Other: Narcissism and the Crisis of Eros
5. The Transparency Society and Infocracy
6. Psychopolitics: From the Control of Bodies to the Control of Minds
7. The Isolated Individual: The Digital Swarm
8. Ways Out: The Salvation of the Beautiful and the Recovery of Rituals
9. Vita Contemplativa: The Rediscovery of Pause and Inaction
10. Conclusion: The Paradox of Contemporary Freedom
